# Trial recipes

Recipes you want to try but have not vetted yet live on the **Trial** list, apart from your
cookbook. They don't clutter the recipe list or the printed cookbook, and they don't get lost:
the **Trial** tab shows how many are waiting (for example "Trial · 7").

## What lands on the trial list

- Recipes imported from a **website**, **text** or a **photo**. The import screen has an
  **Add to Trial** switch if you would rather put one straight in the cookbook.
- Recipes you create from the Trial screen (**Recipe to try**). In the editor, **Save to**
  switches any recipe between **Cookbook** and **Trial**.
- Bulk imports (Paprika, Mealie, Tandoor, Nextcloud Cookbook, Recipe Keeper) go to the cookbook
  unless you switch **Add to Trial** on. Larder backups keep each recipe where it was.

The Trial screen has two groups: **Not made yet** (newest first, with "added 4 months ago") and
**Tried, undecided** (with "tried 2× · last Nov 12").

Searching from the Recipes screen still finds trial recipes; they carry a **Trial** badge. You
can plan them in the meal planner and add them to shopping lists like any other recipe, and
public share links work for them too.

## Deciding

Opening or reading a trial recipe never counts as making it. Nothing changes until you tap
something.

- Under the title, the trial bar shows `TRIAL · not made yet` with **I made this**, and a menu
  with **Move to cookbook** and **Discard**.
- When you leave [cook mode](cook-mode.md) on a trial recipe, Larder asks **How did it go?**
  If you leave before the last step, **Just looking** is the suggested answer. Swiping the sheet
  away, tapping outside it or pressing back also means "just looking": nothing is recorded.
- After a planned day has passed, meal plan entries for trial recipes show a **Did you make it?**
  chip. Ignore it and nothing happens; answer it once and it goes away (**Skipped** if you didn't
  make it).

The answers:

| Answer | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Keep it** | Moves the recipe to the cookbook and counts it as made |
| **Try again** | Keeps it on the trial list and counts it as made |
| **Not for us** | Removes the recipe (with a few seconds to **Undo**) |
| **Just looking** / **Cancel** | Nothing |

You can add a star rating and a short note. The note is added to the recipe's notes as a dated
line, such as `Nov 12: too salty, cut soy by half`. Counts are kept per household and work
offline: answers given on two phones at once both count.
